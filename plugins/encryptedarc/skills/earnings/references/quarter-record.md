# Quarter Records and Reusable Evidence

Use this reference to preserve what was known in a quarter and compare it with subsequent results. A record is researched Markdown, not a new financial source or a forecast generated by the agent.

## 1. Prior record and current evidence

- Resolve company, exchange, ticker and fiscal period before matching a prior record. Read supplied records or the relevant configured report location; do not search unrelated personal files.
- Extract earlier guidance as issued, later revisions, target periods, open analyst questions and monitoring items. If no prior record exists, say `Prior-quarter baseline unavailable`; current analysis may continue.
- Recheck current facts against company originals under the source policy. Past records preserve historical statements but do not prove current prices, results, or amendment status.
- Recheck material prior guidance attribution against its linked original when accessible. If only the prior record is available, label that comparison as prior-record evidence with its verification limit rather than implying the old release/call was inspected again.
- Compare the same metric, target period, unit, scope and reported/adjusted basis. Reconcile restatements explicitly while preserving the prior record's original as-reported snapshot. Do not silently rewrite history to the restated values.

## 2. Guidance and statement register

Keep one entry per metric/target-period/basis or named milestone. Preserve the following material fields, using a table or clearly labelled bullets in the user's language:

- **Statement type:** `Formal guidance`, `Management outlook`, `Aspiration / milestone`, `Management claim`, `Derived`, or `Analysis/Inference`.
- **Issued:** announcement/call date, speaker and role; for written guidance without a named speaker, attribute it to the company rather than inventing a speaker.
- **Target:** metric or event, target period/deadline, value/range/inequality, currency/unit, accounting basis and business scope.
- **Conditions:** retain material qualifications such as expects, aims, may, subject to approval, funding assumptions or capacity constraints. Approximation in a quoted management outlook is part of its uncertainty, not permission to loosen sourced actuals.
- **Original and revisions:** original wording paraphrased faithfully, original range and source; each dated update with its source and what changed. An updated forecast is not a restatement of historical actuals.
- **Actual and assessment:** comparable actual, source and period; assessment and the next check. Leave fields unavailable rather than infer missing values.
- **Source location:** exact document URL plus date/period and page/table, or transcript speaker/timestamp/paragraph when available. For supplied fixtures/files, use the actual file location rather than inventing a URL.

Evaluate original guidance and the latest pre-results revision separately. A result can fall within the original range and below the revised range; retain both findings. Label midpoint calculations and ratios of guidance midpoints as `Derived`, with formulas and inputs; they are not an additional company-issued margin target.

Use neutral actual-versus-target labels: `Within range`, `Above range`, `Below range`, `Not yet due`, `No comparable actual`, or `Insufficient evidence`. For milestones use `Completed` or `Not completed by stated deadline` only when deadline and evidence support it. Above a spending range is not automatically a favorable outcome.

Track version events separately: `Issued`, `Revised`, `Explicitly withdrawn`, or `Not reiterated`. Silence is not proof of withdrawal or failure. Long-term outlooks remain not yet due before their horizon; current-quarter progress is labelled analysis, not a final hit/miss verdict.

## 3. Management remarks and Q&A

Under a separate heading, keep material prepared remarks and analyst Q&A as attributed evidence:

- Topic and why it matters to the quarter or next check.
- Question, analyst/firm when known, and the concern being tested.
- Answer, named executive/role, statement type, target horizon and material conditions.
- Source document/call date and timestamp/paragraph when available.
- What the answer establishes, what remains unanswered, and the observable follow-up indicator. Judgments about evasiveness or changing tone are `Analysis/Inference` supported by the actual statements.

Paraphrase faithfully and keep any verbatim quotations brief. A concise answer must not turn conditional funding language into a guaranteed self-funding claim. If management declines to give a margin target, preserve that absence instead of treating a derived margin as guidance. If no transcript/replay can be inspected, mark Q&A unavailable; an article's summary must be attributed as secondary coverage, not reconstructed as a direct exchange.

## 4. Record format and later visual reuse

Use the report headings in `earnings-framework.md`, with these records embedded where relevant:

1. Identity and cutoff: company/exchange/ticker, fiscal period and quarter-end, research/as-of date, document versions and verification limits.
2. Metric scoreboard: actual values and applicable YoY/QoQ/consensus comparisons, with units, bases, formulas and sources.
3. Separate business/driver topics, earnings quality, cash/capex/funding and balance-sheet evidence.
4. Guidance/statement register, including original guidance, revisions, actual-versus-target checks and new outlooks.
5. Prepared remarks and Q&A topics with attribution and unresolved questions.
6. What changed since the prior record, risks, next-quarter indicators, missing evidence and the educational disclaimer.

Use `##` section headings and `###` topic headings, with one fact or claim per bullet. Each topic block must retain enough local context to reuse on its own: company/period, metric with units/basis, comparison period, statement type, source and relevant caveat. Put exact source links inside that block; a detached source number alone is insufficient for later reuse. Keep reported results, management forecasts, derived calculations and analysis distinguishable in both text and tables.

Fill this topic template directly, localizing the labels. Keep only applicable fields; add separate evidence bullets for material facts instead of joining several claims. The Source bullet uses inline Markdown links with the exact URL/file path inside the parentheses, so copying this block also copies its provenance. Use the same pattern for material table sections, with local period/basis metadata and inline source links in their source cells or immediately below the table.

```markdown
### <Company - specific topic - fiscal period>

- **Context:** <company, target/actual period and relevant units/accounting scope>
- **Evidence - <Reported actual / Formal guidance / other statement type>:** <one material fact or faithful qualified statement; comparison period if used>
- **Question:** <analyst/firm and question, when this is Q&A>
- **Answer - <executive/role and statement type>:** <answer with target horizon and conditions, when this is Q&A>
- **Analysis / next check:** <inference distinguished from evidence, uncertainty and observable follow-up>
- **Source:** [<document, publication date, fiscal period and page/timestamp>](<exact supporting URL or actual supplied file path>)
```

A global source list may supplement these inline links. It does not replace the block's Source bullet. A topic that refers only to a reference-style definition such as `[A]` or `[evidence]` elsewhere in the report is not ready for standalone extraction.

For a later request for infographic content, extract the requested topics from the verified record, retaining the source, as-of date, units, forecast/actual labels and uncertainty. Reverify time-sensitive information before presenting it as current. Do not create images, design briefs, JSON exports or promotional claims merely because the record may later be reused visually.

## 5. Saving and retrieving

- Resolve destination and permission from the current user request, applicable instructions and local extension. A path is not consent if the extension requires approval. Honor existing explicit authorization without asking again; otherwise obtain required save approval at the save step.
- If no destination/permission or file-writing capability is available, deliver structured Markdown in chat at the requested level of detail, state it has not been persistently saved, and identify the missing persistence or baseline input. An explicitly brief chat response is not a claim that a complete archive exists. Do not invent a location or claim future access to chat history.
- When saving is authorized, follow the configured naming convention. Otherwise, within the authorized report directory, use a filename identifying ticker/exchange, fiscal period and research date, such as `Earnings_SIM-EXI_FY2026-Q2_2026-10-30.md`.
- Preserve prior records. If the target filename exists, write a new numbered revision such as `_v2` rather than overwrite it. Record the prior filename/version used and the cutoff of the new record. Update indexes only when configured or requested.
- Do not write records into the installed skill directory or hardcode a personal vault, provider, or OS path. A future run loads a record supplied by the user or available in its configured authorized location; if unavailable, disclose the gap rather than claiming continuity.
